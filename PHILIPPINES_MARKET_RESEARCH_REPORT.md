## 1. Macro Market Context & Demographics

The Philippine retail investing landscape is experiencing a digital leapfrog transition. Historically limited to an elite cohort in Metro Manila, the capital markets are rapidly opening up to mass-market retail participants through mobile e-wallets.

### Empirical Market Ground Truths:
1. **Fintech Integration & Stock Market Democratization**:
   - The Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) reported online trading accounts reaching **1.9+ million registered accounts**.
   - Growth is overwhelmingly powered by frictionless mobile wallet tie-ups: **GStocks PH (GCash)** and **Maya Stocks**.
   - These integrations allow over 70+ million mobile wallet users to open an investment account without minimum initial bank balance barriers.
   - *Source*: Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) Stock Market Investor Profile Report. [Tier 1, Score 9.8/10]
2. **OFW Remittances ($37B+ Annual Volume) & Wealth Transition**:
   - Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) reported annual overseas Filipino worker (OFW) cash remittances exceeding **$37 billion USD**.
   - A generational shift is underway: OFWs increasingly allocate a portion of remittances into income-generating **dividend blue chips and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)** rather than traditional low-interest passbook savings accounts.
   - *Source*: Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Economic & Financial Statistics. [Tier 1, Score 9.7/10]
3. **SEC Philippines Advisory Crackdown on Unlicensed Platforms**:
   - SEC Philippines frequently issues public cease-and-desist warnings against unregistered offshore brokers and Facebook/Telegram "investment syndicates" promising guaranteed weekly interest or crypto yields.
   - The SEC requires all entities offering securities or investment contracts to secure an active secondary license under the Securities Regulation Code (SRC).
   - *Source*: SEC Philippines Investors Education & Information Advisories. [Tier 1, Score 9.6/10]
